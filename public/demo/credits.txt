Composites
The browser segmented each distinct video frame with the bundled MediaPipe model. Per scene, all three composites reuse the frame, placement, foreground outline and sample region. The top-left panel is the scene photograph at the same crop. Output and poster generation: scripts/build_motion_demo.py. Placement, duration, segmentation overlap and decoded frame counts are recorded in verification.json. This demonstration uses public source footage and does not report a participant study.
